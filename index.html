Soap dish math

The fancy bar melts away in three weeks, the cheap one sits there for months, and the pump bottle is a mystery in milliliters. Soap dish math turns grams and washes into the days a bar really lasts, the bars and the money a year of handwashing costs, and the bottle into pumps and days so the bar-versus-bottle argument gets numbers. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Life plan - bar grams and washes become days on the dish.
  • Year plan - days a bar becomes bars and cost per year.
  • Bottle plan - bottle milliliters and pumps become days.

Labeled norms

Labeled bar: 100 g. Labeled wash: 0.5 g of soap. Labeled pump: 2 ml. Labeled household: 6 washes a day. These are reference values, not rules - your soap, your hands and your household decide the real numbers.
